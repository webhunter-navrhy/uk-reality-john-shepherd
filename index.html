* GREAT FOR INVESTORS AND FIRST TIME BUYERS*

Three bedroom end of terraced property with upstairs bathroom and garden.

Situated on Marston Road in Stafford, this spacious three-bedroom end terrace property offers an excellent opportunity for first-time buyers, families, or investors alike. Ideally positioned within easy reach of Stafford town centre, local shops, schools, amenities and Stafford railway station, the property combines generous living space with a highly convenient location. Stafford station is approximately 0.8 miles away, providing excellent commuter links.

Ideal for first-time buyers, investors or those looking for a project, the property provides spacious accommodation throughout together with a good-sized rear garden.

The accommodation briefly comprises: an entrance hallway giving access to the stairs and leading into a generous lounge, separate dining room and fitted kitchen to the rear. To the first floor there are two double bedrooms, single bedroom and a family bathroom located off the landing.

Externally, the property benefits from a rear garden offering outside space, parking is avaliable on street.

WHERE IT IS...

Marston Road is ideally situated within short distance of Stafford town centre, making it a convenient choice for commuters, families and professionals alike. The town centre offers a wide range of shops, supermarkets, cafés, restaurants and leisure facilities, all easily accessible from the property.

For commuters, Stafford railway station is approximately 0.8 miles away and provides regular direct services to Birmingham, Manchester and London Euston, making the property well placed for those travelling for work. The M6 motorway (Junctions 13 and 14) is also readily accessible, offering excellent road links throughout the Midlands and beyond. Stafford Railway Station

The property is particularly well located for families, with a number of well-regarded schools nearby. St Patrick's Catholic Primary School & Nursery is just a short walk away, while John Wheeldon Primary Academy, Tillington Manor Primary School and Sir Graham Balfour School are all within easy reach.
